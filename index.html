🎯 MISSION LOG

Survive 3 waves of enemies in the arena.

Defeat the Final Boss to obtain the Vault Key.

Unlock the Vault to claim your victory.

⌨️ CONTROLS

  • W, A, S, D - Move
  • Mouse - Look / Aim
  • Left Click - Attack / Fire
  • Shift - Dash (Requires Belt)
  • F - Pick up Loot
  • E - Open/Close Inventory
  • O - Interact with Vault

⚙️ SURVIVAL TIPS

Enemies drop scrap and weapons. Press E to open your inventory and equip new prosthetic arms or thruster belts to survive the harder waves!
